It optimises two objectives: an English keyword refusal rate\n      and KL divergence. The selection rule minimises KL subject to refusals &le; 10/100;\n      if no candidate satisfies this, the fallback minimises refusals subject to KL &le; 1.0.\n      Both searches fell to the fallback: no candidate achieved &le; 10 keyword refusals.\n      The keyword objective floor was 72 for Gemma and 16 for GaMS3.\n    </p>\n\n    <h3>Evaluation protocol</h3>\n    <p>\n      All checkpoints are evaluated on the\n      <a href=\"https://github.com/ai-inventor-papers/ai-invention-beaa2a-what-english-tuned-abliteration-misses/tree/fork/run_WY-5q2onKHeA/round-1/dataset-1\">RefusEU benchmark</a>:\n      280 harmful prompts (S5 split) and 150 benign prompts (S6 split), each in English and Slovene.\n      Responses are single greedy-decoded generations (temperature 0, max 256 tokens), classified\n      into refused, partial, or complied by a local Qwen3-14B judge. Attack success rate (ASR)\n      is computed where both Llama Guard 3 and PolyGuard agree. Over-refusal rate is the fraction\n      of benign-prompt responses classified as refused.\n    </p>\n    <p>\n      The 256-token limit truncates 94-95% of edited English outputs.\n      The Qwen3-14B judge was calibrated against GPT-4.1: English &kappa; = 0.86 (gate met);\n      Slovene &kappa; = 0.72 (gate not met). It optimises two objectives: an English keyword refusal rate\n      and KL divergence. The selection rule minimises KL subject to refusals &le; 10/100;\n      if no candidate satisfies this, the fallback minimises refusals subject to KL &le; 1.0.\n      Both searches fell to the fallback: no candidate achieved &le; 10 keyword refusals.\n      The keyword objective floor was 72 for Gemma and 16 for GaMS3.\n    </p>\n\n    <h3>Evaluation protocol</h3>\n    <p>\n      All checkpoints are evaluated on the\n      <a href=\"https://github.com/ai-inventor-papers/ai-invention-beaa2a-what-english-tuned-abliteration-misses/tree/fork/run_WY-5q2onKHeA/round-1/dataset-1\">RefusEU benchmark</a>:\n      280 harmful prompts (S5 split) and 150 benign prompts (S6 split), each in English and Slovene.\n      Responses are single greedy-decoded generations (temperature 0, max 256 tokens), classified\n      into refused, partial, or complied by a local Qwen3-14B judge. Attack success rate (ASR)\n      is computed where both Llama Guard 3 and PolyGuard agree. Over-refusal rate is the fraction\n      of benign-prompt responses classified as refused.\n    </p>\n    <p>\n      The 256-token limit truncates 94-95% of edited English outputs.\n      The Qwen3-14B judge was calibrated against GPT-4.1: English &kappa; = 0.86 (gate met);\n      Slovene &kappa; = 0.72 (gate not met). All Slovene absolute rates carry a measurement caveat.\n      The depth-experiment judge also missed its gate (&kappa; = 0.683).\n    </p>\n  </section>\n\n  <!-- RESULTS -->\n  <section
