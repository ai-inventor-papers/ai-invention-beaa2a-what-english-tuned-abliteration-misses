An English Keyword Objective Is Blind to Its Own Edit in Slovene

Abliteration removes refusal from a language model by editing its weights, and the Heretic optimiser checks each candidate edit with an English keyword counter. 1 · The finding

The counter that picks the edit cannot hear Slovene

The question. searches for an edit and keeps the candidate its scores best. The counter looks for 33 English refusal phrases. When the edited model is asked the same harmful question in Slovene, what does that counter report?

The answer. Nothing at all. On 100 , the counter fires on 87% of the edited Gemma model's English responses and on 0% of its Slovene responses. A reading the same responses finds 13% English refusal and 82% Slovene refusal. The counter's view of the language gap has the wrong sign.

−1.56 for Gemma-edit, 95% CI [−1.68, −1.44]

Keyword-verified abliterated checkpoints are therefore not valid language-neutral references for refusal evaluation. Switch the checkpoint in the chart: the counter is silent on Slovene for every one of the five, including the unedited models that refuse almost everything.

5 · Where it fails

Silent in Slovene, misfiring on edited English

On held-out prompts, when does the counter agree with the judge?

Held-out-category StrongREJECT prompts, disjoint from the search prompts. “Edited” pools the seven edited Gemma arms of the selection study (490 responses per language); “original” is unedited Gemma (70).

What the paper does not show

  • The Slovene judge is not certified. The Qwen3-14B workhorse judge misses the κ ≥ 0.80 gate for Slovene (unweighted κ = 0.72, CI [0.63, 0.81]); English passes at 0.86. Absolute Slovene rates carry this caveat.
  • Most edited outputs are truncated. 94–95% of edited English outputs hit the 256-token limit, so the judge often labelled incomplete text. The truncation metric in the transfer view shows this per cell.
  • Two models, one architecture family. Gemma-3-12B-IT and its Slovene continual-pretraining variant GaMS3. Nothing here attributes the asymmetry to continual pretraining or any other training stage.
  • Depth placement does not travel across models. The activation-space depth index fails cross-model prediction: Spearman ρ = −0.009 over 21 rows (CI [−0.13, 0.19], permutation p = 0.16) between Gemma and Qwen3-8B.
  • NF4 and one configuration. All checkpoints are 4-bit NF4; each model had one Heretic search of 116 TPE trials. One target language.
