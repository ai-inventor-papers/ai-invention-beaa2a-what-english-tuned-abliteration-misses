### 3.2 Layer Selection Strategies

Layer selection is a critical element of the process. Arditi et al. found that abliterating a narrow local region of 2–3 middle layers can be as effective as ablating across all layers, and applied their technique to all layers’ WOW_{O} and WoutW_{\text{out}} plus the embedding matrix. We found that this does not generalize to the broader set of modern models in our study: refusal in many architectures is distributed more widely, particularly in MoE models with hundreds of experts per layer.
